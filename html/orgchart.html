Org Chart

Every department, one section per department, collapsed to head + direct reports by default — for printing the whole company org chart in one go. Each section auto-fits the page width. Each department starts on a new printed page.

Generated from raw_docs/Orgchart.csv (Masterlist export). Every report-count badge shows that person's TOTAL subordinates at every level down (not just direct reports), broken down by job type (IDL / DLS / DL) — this count never changes with the collapse/expand state, which only controls what's rendered on screen. Direct Labor (DL) operators are always counted but never get their own card — a dashed "(not shown)" badge appears instead wherever every one of a person's direct reports is DL. Click a card's button to expand its team; "Collapse to level 1" resets a view to the head and direct reports only.
